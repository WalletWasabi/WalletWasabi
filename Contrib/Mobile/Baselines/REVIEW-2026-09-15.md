## Review scope

The 162 new cases comprise 36 bound Receive/Request Amount captures, eight bound
regtest passphrase authorization captures, 116 unbound layout captures, and two
synthetic native component references. Together with the unchanged 73 earlier
approvals, the corpus contains 235 cases. Every new image was visually inspected
at its viewport and theme. Inspection included both themes, narrow layouts,
validation errors, unavailable QR, authorization rejection and scrolled QR states.

Review found and led to fixes for positive backup-validation claims when bindings
were unavailable, and conflicting transaction-confirmation labels when transaction
data was absent. Positive validation now fails closed and unavailable confirmation
status remains neutral. Compact-phone content can scroll vertically; the complete
QR can be brought into view and decoded from the actual rendered framebuffer, and
the copy button is reachable and copies the current payload.

Receive captures use the production native surface, presentation state and QR
encoder with a deterministic regtest address. Authorization captures exercise the
production password verifier through the production command and compiled view,
reject incorrect input, and complete the dialog only after a correct retry. The
wallet is isolated and never started, funded, signed or broadcast.

Unbound images are explicitly layout evidence, not successful wallet operations.
No recovery phrases or user-wallet data are rendered. This review does not certify
original design-board bitmap parity, physical-device behavior, hardware-wallet
transport, end-to-end funded transactions, or release readiness.

## Acceptance policy

Each approval records dimensions plus the exact decoded RGBA fingerprint. No masks,
resizing or tolerant comparison are applied. Every emitted PNG requires a validated
manifest. The workflow checks nonempty complete TRX results, all reviewed captures,
and exact cross-process repetition. CI never enrolls or replaces its own baselines.
See `../VISUAL-TESTING.md` for reproduction and review procedures.
